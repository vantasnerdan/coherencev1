# Bulk PdD launch constructions

All preparations use the same nearest-D target, the same periodic register and the same excess energy $E=Ne$. `patterns.py` consumes the actual positions and orthorhombic box supplied by `engine.py` from `g10_quantum_site/model.py:full('Pd')` and its periodic tiling. The engine owns forces, support and propagation ([retained inputs](keep-drop.md)). The zero-displacement launches use $s=\sqrt{2e/m_D}$ in Å/fs, with $\sum_i|v_i|^2=Ns^2$. The prescribed bond axis $n$ points from row $i$ to row $j$ in the nearest image. No field or method of realizing these launches is assumed.

| Family | Preparation, before propagation |
|---|---|
| Converging waves | Broad superposition of longitudinal reciprocal waves in the actual box. Use $\sin[k\cdot(r-r_{ij})]\hat k$ about the bond midpoint, with $0<|k|d\le\pi$, $\hat k\cdot n>0.45$ and $d$ the equilibrium bond length. Opposite reciprocal vectors are the same real mode; at most 16 distinct modes are retained, sampling the admissible magnitudes in sorted order. The target rows both vanish by projecting out the launch target in coefficient space. Project the delayed harmonic readout onto this subspace and normalize to $E$; its signed modal phases add toward inward response at the proposed time. Each zero-displacement mode is a standing superposition of oppositely traveling longitudinal constituents, not a claimed purely incoming pulse. The phase matching is harmonic; convergence and hold in the connected nonlinear model are measured, not assumed. |
| Two approaching packets | Place two compact kinetic envelopes about $r_{ij}\pm0.95dn$ using periodic nearest positions. Each has longitudinal and transverse radii $0.36d$ and $1.05d$, with squared raised-polynomial weight $(1-\rho^2)^2$ inside. They are disjoint and point toward one another along $n$, each with $E/2$. The target rows are silent. There are four driven D rows on each side on this register, so this launch is already concentrated on eight sites, independent of $N$. It seeds opposing excitations; lattice propagation, not ballistic particle motion, determines whether their encounter actually loads the target. |
| Reverse of spreading | Seed the *outward* target velocities $v_i=-s\sqrt{N/2}\,n$, $v_j=-v_i$, others zero. Evolve without loss to the assigned preparation delay (up to half the forward comparison horizon, shortened to the last supported state if necessary). Reverse by keeping the actual displacements and negating every velocity. That displaced state is the scored launch at energy $E$ up to integration drift. Declare its actual preparation time, support, launch participation and loading. A return from this prepared state is a reachability control, not freely realizable pumping or independent evidence at $t=0$. |
| Direct launch optimization | Non-target site velocities solve the capped harmonic adjoint problem at each proposed time; $v_i=v_j=0$. Nonlinear scoring then compares these time trials and bounded phase/amplitude refinements within the same energy and target constraints. `direct_refinements` rotates by $\pm12^\circ$ about each of two transverse poles without changing any site energy. `capped_direct_velocity` also applies to mixtures of the delayed adjoints/velocity profiles. Selection is over the finite nonlinear trial set, not a global nonlinear optimum. |

The separate `preload` control is the *inward* version of the two-site seed, not a focusing strategy. With $\mu=m_D/2$, $K_{\rm rel}=\mu|v_i-v_j|^2/2$, and this control has $K_{\rm rel}(0)=E$, effective kinetic participation 2 and signed inward speed positive. The outward seed has the same $K_{\rm rel}(0)$ with the opposite sign. For a lossless, time-independent velocity-even Hamiltonian in support, $\Theta(q,v)=(q,-v)$ gives $\Phi_\tau\Theta\Phi_\tau=\Theta$; the sign-flipped trajectory therefore returns to the inward seed after the actual preparation delay $\tau$, up to numerical error. The identity alone does not establish spreading. For a displaced reverse launch use positive site energies $K_\ell+V_{\rm local}(q_\ell)$ (exclude signed intersite terms from this diagnostic). To count as distributed reversal, their effective participation must be at least $N/4$ and the two target sites' share below 5%. If the endpoint fails this criterion, retain its return as a preload/reachability control, not evidence for spreading. Kinetic-only participation cannot replace this criterion at a turning point.

## Exact harmonic proposal, finite nonlinear readout

Let $Bv=(v_i-v_j)\cdot n$. For the accepted equal-mass harmonic Hessian $H$, $v(t)=\cos(t\sqrt{H/m_D})v(0)$ when $q(0)=0$. The signed scalar response has one row $h(t)^T=B\cos(t\sqrt{H/m_D})$. For the unrestricted relative velocity vector the output rank is at most three; squared energy alone loses sign and direction. The callback `predicted_kernel(t)` provides the adjoint $h(t)$ in shape `(N,3)` at a finite time in fs; its dot product with the launch gives speed in Å/fs, not energy. A uniform velocity $v_\Gamma$ stays uniform in the harmonic model, so $Bv_\Gamma=0$: a uniform mean consumes energy without helping this linear readout. This decoupling need not hold in the nonlinear model. Inward signs need not stay positive throughout the run. The final radial projection uses the instantaneous pair axis and distinguishes inward and outward at the measured total-relative-kinetic peak.

For direct launches, $a_\ell=\|h_\ell\|$, $r_\ell=\|v_\ell\|$ on the $N-2$ free sites. Align each nonzero-coefficient velocity with its local adjoint, reducing the objective to $\sum_\ell a_\ell r_\ell$. The exact energy constraint is $\sum_\ell r_\ell^2=Ns^2$; the default speed cap is $c=2s\sqrt{N/(N-2)}$ (four times the mean *kinetic energy on free sites*). Where the nonzero coefficients can carry all the energy, the optimum is $r_\ell^*=\min(c,\alpha a_\ell)$ with $\alpha$ fixed by the energy constraint. At a saturated site, $a_\ell\ge r_\ell^*/\alpha$ and $r_\ell-r_\ell^*\le0$; at each unsaturated site there is equality in the coefficient relation. Thus the objective difference to any feasible trial is bounded above by $r^*\cdot(r-r^*)/\alpha=-\|r-r^*\|^2/(2\alpha)\le0$. If all nonzero coefficients can saturate without using up the budget, saturate them and fill the residual equally on null sites in deterministic alternating transverse directions; they cannot change the objective. At `cap_multiplier=1`, every non-target site must have the same speed, with the same signed phase rule. A cap of $s$ with target sites silent would violate the fixed energy budget.

At launch the effective kinetic participation is $P=(\sum |v_\ell|^2)^2/\sum |v_\ell|^4$; uniform fraction is $N|\bar v|^2/\sum |v_\ell|^2$. The default direct cap implies $P\ge(N-2)/4$ since $\sum |v_\ell|^4\le c^2\sum |v_\ell|^2$. Distinguish the instantaneous relative kinetic fraction $|v_i-v_j|^2/(2\sum |v_\ell|^2)$ from the two target sites' kinetic fraction; they coincide only when those velocities are equal and opposite. At a supported peak report $K_{\rm rel}/e$, total fraction $K_{\rm rel}/E$, inward signed speed, radial component, separation, time and the contiguous half-peak hold. This hold is an energy-duration diagnostic, not persistence of positive inward velocity. Keep the prescribed target and the best bond anywhere on the same trajectory as separate readouts; a larger whole-cell maximum is not improved target delivery. No extremum beyond the first support exit is admissible.

## Interface and controls

`velocity_candidates(kind, positions_A, box_A, target, axis, speeds_A_per_fs, predicted_kernel=None, *, focus_times_fs=())` yields dictionaries with `name`, `q`, `v`, `role`, `focus_time_fs` and launch diagnostics. The `q,v` arrays have shape `(N,3)`; all constructor `q` are zero, and the engine externally builds the actual displaced reverse state. `wave` and `direct` use explicit positive focus times from the consumer; a time with no resolved projected wave signal is not a matched wave proposal. `collision`, `reverse_seed`, and `preload` each yield one state. Candidate choice and matched nonlinear acceptance traces are in `run.py` and its `results.json`, `comparison.csv`, `launches.npz`, and `trajectories.npz` outputs (run with `python run.py --run` in the configured NumPy/SciPy/Numba environment).

A construction-only check at 250 fs used the actual engine register and harmonic adjoint at all three sizes. Each family had the stated total velocity norm; wave/direct/collision had exact zero target velocities, wave/direct had positive proposed inward speed, and direct respected its cap, including under the four angle refinements. A sparse signed coefficient control exercised saturated nonzero coefficients, orthogonal null-energy fill and the equal-speed limit. No nonlinear propagation was used to obtain the launch checks below; these values are not selected trajectory results.
