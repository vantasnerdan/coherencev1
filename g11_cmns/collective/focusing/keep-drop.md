# Bulk PdD focusing: keep and drop

**Question:** At the same total excess energy, does a spatially distributed preparation concentrate more energy on a single D–D relative coordinate as the cell grows, or do dispersion and nonlinear forces stop the increase? Allocation to the target at launch is a ceiling/control, not evidence of focusing.

**Keep.** The fully occupied periodic PdD D register, physical deuteron mass, accepted 0.80 Å G10 local well, and Part 1 small-displacement K/F₀ and folded harmonic couplings. Tile the actual geometry to 32, 256 and 2048 D. When lifting a folded bond, split it among its equal shortest images so refolding at 32 exactly restores the old graph; do not treat the folded value as an independent infinite-crystal force constant. Keep the measured five-row connected W and its signed force on the symmetric nearest-neighbour inward path. As the simplest default off-path closure, retain the old harmonic energy and replace its inward part by adding W(q)−Jₙₙq², q²=max(n·xᵢ,0) max(−n·xⱼ,0). The correction vanishes for a one-site displacement and matches the symmetric cut. The 0-to-0.12 Å segment must match the established old quadratic coefficient near zero and E, dE/du at the first nonzero anchor. Three rays and one connected cut are not measured angular coverage. Keep the known alternate H-treatment point as one sentence only; no rerun of contact.

**Why keep the old linear limit.** An unconstrained cubic through W(0)=W′(0)=0 and the first native anchor gives W≈0.140884u² eV, versus Jₙₙ=0.018874 eV/Å². Promoting that curvature to every nearest-neighbour longitudinal bond while leaving the rest of the graph unchanged makes the Γ curvature negative (about −0.286 eV/Å²). The .12 Å finite-displacement point does not independently determine curvature at zero. Matching it without inventing that small-signal instability is required; the initial spectral check must exercise the actual tiled graph.

**Compare.** Fixed total energies N×(0.6, 4.8, 25) meV. Record how concentrated each launch already is, finite target time, and its measured peak relative kinetic energy μ|vᵢ−vⱼ|²/2 together with the same-time separation, inward longitudinal component, and contiguous half-peak duration. Distinguish behavior on the prescribed target bond from the best bond anywhere. Use one fixed loss sensitivity, not a fit or sweep. Report the first site-radius >.80 Å or any nearest-pair R<1.287824 Å and do not use later extrema as supported performance. Flag the .48/.80 connected PAW points separately from a domain exit.

**Drop.** Dynamic Pd/electronic degrees of freedom, thermal bath/continuous forcing or a guessed pump field, defects, surfaces, other hosts, and unsupported force extrapolation as evidence. Use the existing normalized radial per-level contact curves only as a conditional energy readout; a classical peak does not specify a radial quantum state, its population, an event-integrated contact or a device/foil rate. Keep model-active-pair G12 units separate from detected counts. Extrapolate 100 eV/1 keV only if an observed scaling supports a stated conditional law; always give the total-energy lower bounds and mark where the force and contact models stop.
