# CMNS models: what they predict for ordinary D–D

**NG** = no number given in the cited model for that observable. **p/n** means the ordinary `p+t` to `n+³He` yield ratio. A channel energy, fitted partial width, or rate conditional on a prepared state is not a beam-foil yield. Measurements in the last column are proposed discriminators, not validations.

| Model and primary source | Ordinary D–D branches and ⁴He | Needed inputs; already in Coherencev1 | One measurement |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hagelstein** — A relativistic boost couples lattice momentum to nuclear internal transitions. A shared oscillator can exchange many quanta or transfer excitation between nuclei; asymmetric loss or off-resonant level shifts can reduce destructive interference. [Boost, App. F](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6455/acf3be) · [Lossy model, §3](https://jcmns.scholasticahq.com/api/v1/articles/72149-energy-exchange-in-the-lossy-spin-boson-model.pdf) · [Up-conversion, §§1, 6–7](https://arxiv.org/abs/0709.3557) · [Transfer, abstract](https://jcmns.org/article/72481-phonon-mediated-nuclear-excitation-transfer/attachment/150510.pdf). | **p+t, n+³He, p/n:** NG. **⁴He:** proposes routing ~24 MeV from D₂→⁴He via transfer and subdivision; NG for foil yield ([2022, abstract and §§1, 8](https://jcmns.org/article/72598-recent-progress-on-phonon-nuclear-theoretical-models.pdf)). The spin-one example exchanges 15 oscillator quanta at dressed resonance, not a keV delivery rate in PdD. The [⁵⁷Co-source study, abstract](https://jcmns.org/article/72479-observation-of-non-exponential-decay-in-x-ray-and-emission-lines-from-co-57/attachment/150508.pdf) reports +19% in the 14.4-keV ⁵⁷Fe line and +17% Fe Kα, but not the sought ultrasound-induced transfer; the nuclear excitation came from decay. | Mode energy, occupation, coherent population, nuclear matrix elements, detuning and loss/dephasing. Available: PdD force constants and 35-meV single-D harmonic quantum ([g3_loading](../../g3_loading/README.md)), modeled driven patterns ([g3_lattice](../../g3_lattice/README.md)); no foil mode occupation, effective nuclear N, D–D coupling, or applicable loss spectrum. | Drive-synchronous p and n energy spectra at fixed beam energy and loading, with mode occupation measured. |
| **Bressani–Del Giudice–Preparata** — A coherent electronic plasma lowers the D–D barrier. An equilibrium D coherence domain is proposed to transfer the ⁴He* de-excitation energy collectively to metal electrons. [1989, pp. 847–849, Eqs. 6–12](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02844877). | **p+t, n+³He, p/n:** NG separately. The paper estimates ~10⁹ suppression of the **combined** nuclear channels relative to its electromagnetic channel at equilibrium and D/metal ≈1; it does not apply that claim to nonequilibrium loading. **⁴He:** ~23.8 MeV to the medium per proposed event; NG for an incident-ion yield. | Loading, electron density, coherence length/population and penetration factor. Available: [fixed loading profiles](../../g4_beam/merit/README.md) and [electronic response](../../g6_response/README.md), not the model's nuclear coherence domain. | Heat-correlated ⁴He yield resolved against an independently measured coherence signal at fixed loading. |
| **Czerski et al.** — A near-threshold 0⁺ state in ⁴He interferes with broader 0⁺ amplitudes in ordinary D–D and can decay by internal pair creation (IPC). The ion-track extension adds a thermal population. [2022, Fig. 1 and Eqs. 3–6](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevC.106.L011601) · [track model, v3, Eqs. 7–9, 14–17](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.02112v3). | **p+t:** screened broad-plus-resonant amplitude; Zr fit Γₚ=40±15 meV; v3 uses 40 meV and 115°. **n+³He, p/n:** NG for the sub-keV resonance; the later beam analysis **assumes** p/n≈1. **⁴He:** 2022 extrapolates ≥100 IPC per proton at room temperature; [Dubey et al., §§III–IV and App. A](https://doi.org/10.1103/chlp-b215) fit ΓIPC/Γₚ=14 and predict IPC dominance below 5 keV beam energy. The fitted width ratio is not an emitted yield ratio. | Level position, widths/phases, stopping, screening, and track population/lifetime if thermal. [g4_beam](../../g4_beam/czerski/README.md) carries the fixed proton term, a Bosch–Hale neutron baseline and a fitted thermal product; it does not determine the neutron resonance width or track state. | Energy-dependent IPC-to-proton yield with identification of the positron/annihilation signature. |
| **Takahashi** — A tetrahedral four-D/electron cluster contracts under maintained symmetry; a time-dependent effective potential and extrapolated short-range rate estimate simultaneous four-D fusion. [2009, §§1–2 and 4](https://doi.org/10.70923/001c.72113). | **p+t, n+³He, p/n:** NG; ordinary two-D fusion is described as negligible within the assumed symmetric cluster. **⁴He:** nearly 100% four-D fusion *per formed TSC* in the calculation; two 23.8-MeV α particles are proposed, with final-state qualifications. NG for the absolute TSC formation rate. | Four-D geometry and formation rate, electron dynamics, short-range absorption matrix element. Available: site geometry and local wells in [g3_loading](../../g3_loading/README.md), not the population or lifetime of a collapsing TSC. | Coincident, correlated α-pair spectrum for the proposed four-D final state. |
| **Kim** — Mobile deuterons in micro/nanograins occupy a proposed Bose ground state; an approximate equal-trap-frequency charge-to-mass rule favors ⁴He and shares energy with the other deuterons. [2009, pp. 805–809, Eqs. 5–6, 14](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00114-009-0537-6). | **p+t, n+³He:** described as suppressed or forbidden under the approximate rule; numbers and **p/n** NG. **⁴He:** favored, Q=23.84 MeV; the modeled per-trap reaction rate scales as ΩN²/Dₜᵣₐₚ³, not an absolute ⁴He branch yield (NG). | Trap size/occupancy, mobile-D density, Bose occupation Ω and nuclear S factor. Available: foil loading and [bulk bound-D distributions](../../g10_quantum_site/README.md), not a measured mobile condensate fraction or foil grain trap population. | ⁴He production versus measured grain size at matched loading: the paper proposes total-rate slopes of −1 or −6 under different Ω assumptions (p. 809). |
| **Schwinger** — In a phonon-vacuum lattice model, D+D populates the first excited 0⁺ state of ⁴He, transferring ~3.8 MeV to the lattice. The level decays to p+t; n+³He is closed for that level. [1990, PDF pp. 2–4 and 8–9](https://lenr-canr.org/acrobat/SchwingerJnuclearene.pdf). | **p+t:** phonon-fed tritium path gives ~10⁻¹⁹ or ~10⁻¹² s⁻¹ per pair at the illustrative 0.94-Å spacing and 0.10 or 0.125 Å zero-point displacements. **Ordinary beam p+t/n+³He and p/n:** NG. **Stable ⁴He:** NG for this D+D path; the separate [p+D proposal](https://lenr-canr.org/acrobat/SchwingerJcoldfusionb.pdf) is not this branch. | Close-pair population/separation, displacement distribution, phonon spectrum and nuclear overlap. Available: [bulk single-site distributions](../../g10_quantum_site/README.md) and wells, not the lattice-conditioned close-pair overlap in the model. | A low combined p+t kinetic-energy band associated with the proposed first-0⁺ decay, distinct from the ordinary beam branch. |

**Three largest omissions:** (1) No common branch-resolved sub-keV foil dataset links these source regimes. (2) The actual driven mode occupation, coherent nuclear population and loss spectrum are unmeasured. (3) Most cited models lack a quantitative bridge from the prepared state or local transition to ordinary p+t and n+³He yields at the fixed foil profile.
