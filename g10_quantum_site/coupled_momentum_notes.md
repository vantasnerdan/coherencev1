# Coupled momentum estimator

`displaced_path` takes ordered primitive closed-path coordinates in Å, the selected site's isotope mass and temperature, and either the total bead PES or its exact selected-site polynomial and fixed-graph neighbour field. Its displacement variable is conjugate to `k=p/ℏ`; it does not use dynamical or thermostat velocities. The midpoint shift (Lin et al., [PRL 105, 110602](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.105.110602), i-PI 3.3.1 `displacedpath`) is exact for the positive free-end primitive operator at finite bead count. The optional harmonic bridge is only a variance-reduction change of variables. The finite-bead momentum diagonal contains a free-end factor `exp(-β Eₖ/P)` before normalization, so report bead-count sensitivity separately from sampling error.

Use `radial_momentum` for `dP/d|k|`, `momentum_component` for the signed projection (for example `[111]`), and `radial_survival` for the integrated radial tail. Fourier bins can be negative; none are clipped or renormalized. The mesh/window deltas and block error describe numerical detection, not a certified far-tail precision. The extent of a computed coordinate-space fit (0.24 Å for the adopted PdD well) does not map to a pointwise cutoff in momentum: display the spread across continuations separately, using the same variant PES for both the shifted paths and any whole-frame log reweights.

Synthetic harmonic control: maximum absolute characteristic difference `8.9e-16` versus the exact finite-bead Gaussian, radial difference `1.43e-16`; signed projection and integrated survival also matched. Weighted mixed-quartic and neighbour-coupled block estimates agreed with the direct whole-bead PES callback within `3.33e-16`. A separate thermal quartic comparison to finite-grid spatial diagonalization **did not** meet its continuum tolerance (`5 SE + 5e-4`): at 48 beads the maximum characteristic difference was `0.00330` and maximum block SE `0.000583`. Bead bias and reweighting uncertainty were not separated. These are synthetic controls, not bulk-cell results.

The three physical omissions are electron–phonon damping, real-time dynamics (the paths describe equilibrium), and any change of the well during ion arrival. Input/PES continuation and numerical uncertainties are separate from those omissions.
