# G10 — PdD pair sensitivity supplement

Companion to the integrated five-point PdD pair cut (`g10_quantum_site/pdd-nearest-pair/`, original draft asset 790324076). No new points replace that curve. The two comparisons are at **different D–D separations**.

- **1.288 Å, H representation only.** Replace standard H PAW with GPAW's projector-free, regularized-Coulomb H; keep Pd10, 500 eV plane waves, Γ-centred 3×3×3 sampling and the frozen geometry. The total approach energy goes from 1.390325 to 1.473687 eV (**+0.083362 eV**). The connected contribution after subtracting the two matched single-site increments goes from 0.376384 to 0.371642 eV (**−0.004742 eV**). Its path derivative with respect to inward motion of each D goes from +1.716788 to +1.758103 eV/Å. Each treatment uses its own zero, D25-only and simultaneous D25/D63 displacement; the D63-only value and vector forces use the exact periodic midpoint inversion. This is a finite-cutoff representation comparison, not a bound on overlap error.
- **1.928 Å, mesh only.** At the same geometries, standard PAW and 500 eV cutoff, going from Γ-centred 3×3×3 to 4×4×4 changes the total approach energy from 0.316033 to 0.319395 eV (**+0.003362 eV**), with a maximum absolute force-component difference of 0.007363 eV/Å at the displaced geometry. This passes the stated 0.005 eV/0.02 eV/Å check at **1.928 Å only**. The finer-mesh calculation at 1.288 Å did not reach the force criterion. No finer-mesh connected difference was computed at either distance.

`pair-supplement.json` binds both comparisons and the original signed [111] context. The complete matched source JSON files, their member hashes and the replay scripts are included. Replay from this directory with `python replay/readout_pair_additions.py bulk-native pair-native mesh-native projector-free-native references <output file> --mesh-pair r1928`. The electronic energies are force-consistent free energies relative to each treatment's own zero; all Cartesian forces preserve the original atom order.
