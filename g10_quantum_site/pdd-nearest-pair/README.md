# G10 — Two neighbouring deuterons approaching in bulk PdD

This cut moves original deuterons **25 and 63** (zero-based) equal distances in opposite directions along their [110] separation in the adopted, fully loaded bulk PdD cell. The initial D–D distance is 2.887824 Å; `u_A` is the distance each ion moves in Å, so `separation_A` decreases by `2u_A`. The original periodic image of 63 has lattice shift `[0, 0, 0]`. Every other ion and the cell are held fixed in all calculations. The adoption and native settings are the same as for the bulk single-site wells. This is a static Born–Oppenheimer finite-displacement cut, not a motion simulation or reaction calculation.

- `pair-wells.json` contains the matched one-ion and two-ion electronic free energies, actual positions, and full forces in original atom order. At each separation, `one_A` moves only deuteron 25, `one_B` moves only 63, and `pair_AB` moves both. The exact G3 zero is in `references/Pd_zero.json`. The independently computed site-B point at 0.24 Å checks the periodic midpoint inversion used to derive the remaining B rows from A. The underlying computed controls and pair rows are separately hash-bound in `SHA256SUMS`.
- `pair_nonadditive_energy_eV` is the pair energy minus both one-ion increments: E(AB) − E(A) − E(B) + E(0). A positive number means the constrained approach costs more than the sum of the two single-site costs. A negative number means it costs less; neither sign alone establishes attraction or a bound state.
- `pair_force_on_A_eV_A` and `pair_force_on_B_eV_A` subtract the appropriate one-body force at the corresponding displaced atom. Their signed axial difference gives the negative of `d_pair_energy_du_eV_A`. `all_atom_force_mixed_difference_eV_A` is a different observable: a change in the full force field at fixed original indices when both displacements are present. Do not project it as the derivative of the cut or add it on top of the two site corrections.

The pair cut alone does not identify a general vector pair potential or its thermal weight. Each zero, one-ion or two-ion geometry has `minimum_periodic_Pd_D_A` and a flag for falling below the sum of the maximum Pd and H valence partial-wave radii (2.5 and 0.9 bohr, about 1.7992 Å; exact setup digests are in `paw_support`). For the pair cut, overlap starts beyond 0.36 Å displacement per site in the sampled set. Flagged values describe this fixed PAW model in a compressed geometry; absence of overlap is not a numerical or physical accuracy bound.

`pair-additions.json` holds the frozen **single**-D [111] ray from the accepted 0.80 Å anchor to the unoccupied geometrical tetrahedral site, 1.768424 Å from the original octahedron, and the independent Γ-centred 4×4×4 mesh check at pair zero and the 1.287824 Å separation. Intermediate ray energies and signed forces constrain a barrier only if they bracket it; no relaxed minimum-energy path is implied. With only two geometries, the mesh check compares the total pair approach energy and forces, not the connected term at the second mesh. Blank contrast and `missing_native_cases`, if present, mean no numerical comparison is claimed for unsaved points.

The second H treatment replaces standard H PAW with GPAW's projector-free regularized-Coulomb H and leaves the Pd PAW, geometry, k3 mesh, and 500 eV cutoff unchanged. Its own zero, single displacement and pair displacement define the total and connected approach energies, with the second single energy obtained by midpoint inversion. `pair_projector_free_contrast` reports the two second-minus-standard energy differences if all required geometries were admitted. That is a finite-basis treatment sensitivity, not a bound on the error caused by overlap alone.

## What was resolved

`pair-cut.csv` keeps all five calculated separations and has a dedicated overlap column. The connected energy is positive at all five points. It rises from 0.00271 eV at 2.648 Å to 0.03389 eV at 2.168 Å, the shortest sampled point without nominal overlap. The flagged 1.928 and 1.288 Å rows give 0.07367 and 0.37638 eV. Keep the flagged points distinct when using the short end; this cut does not provide a non-overlap comparison at 1.0–1.6 Å.

The fixed [111] energies at 0.80, 1.10, 1.40 and 1.768424 Å are 0.30059, 0.52992, 0.67778 and 0.81251 eV above the octahedral zero; the force component still points back at each sample. These samples do not bracket a barrier or show a minimum at the exact tetrahedral coordinate. All four are flagged by the nominal Pd–D overlap diagnostic.

**Unresolved comparisons:** The finer-mesh closest SCF stopped before the force criterion was met; only its zero was admitted. The projector-free calculation admitted zero and one displaced D but did not start the closest two-D calculation before the stop. Neither the finer-mesh difference nor the independent H-treatment differences (total and connected) are known; none are reported as zero. The completed reference rows are included for both checks.
