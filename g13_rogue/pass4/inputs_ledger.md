## Coarse receiver meaning

Free-face guided thickness eigenfunctions are mass normalized, so their surface and depth projections have a defined energy metric. At larger thickness-mode counts a bulk receiver Green approximation is used separately from that low-k kernel. Straight receiver-driven characteristics integrate directional group velocity through the actual grain map; a scalar same-branch impedance split conserves `R+T=1`. This is a weak-interface, no-mode-conversion approximation, not an exact anisotropic Snell/focusing solution. Reflected grain energy is a conservative redistribution remainder, not a second absorption coefficient. Global residence/edge escape is explicitly conditional; unknown mount impedance is not fitted.

The first attempt exposed a **representation error**, not a need for larger random arrays: a far-field `1/r²` Green intensity extended into the subwavelength track core makes the weighted participation spuriously small while its support count is billions. The retained coarse construction averages over one acoustic resolution length `1/k` and the declared source radius, using `r²+a²+k^-2` (and its guided cylindrical counterpart). It reports a finite-resolution acoustic energy density, not an atomically local singular energy. Packet support also includes depth/deposition travel spread, rather than declaring its duration to be `1/f` in every band. This route still covers the whole foil and every requested band.

Sparse fields retain all same-parent echoes and shared neighbouring receiver paths from the outset, beside parent-phase shuffling. Energy-only dense arms retain correlated compound-Poisson moments and empirical conditional samples, not a universal Rayleigh law. Count, participation, signed field, impact rate, receiver event rate and microscopic energy transfer remain separate. Finite packet support and finite record/cell exposure, zero records and censored width/duration are explicit.

`band_depth_full_support.csv/npz` uses one parent measure jointly across28 band/depth cells, bounded to first-five-image/eight-sigma fronts. Its all-modeled-front ancestry is separate from the12-energy-length importance census, finite source-history bound and weighted participation. The source history is finite, not an infinite exact count. Omitted temporal Gaussian energy is included separately from spatial attenuation censoring.

Signed catalogues are band-conditioned finite realizations; there is no reconstructed all-band signed trajectory. The three complex branch channels are a mode-diagonal energy envelope, not a common microscopic tensor basis. Neighbouring receivers do share those same parent paths. Peak/hole labels use earlier-parent-only energy at the nearest stored sample to first-envelope onset; the anchor time and sampling grid remain available, with out-of-record labels censored.

Bulk launch integrates the projected momentum/eigenstress source and acoustic density of states. Its branch-integrated work is then consumed by a **coarse normalized normal-impulse angular ansatz**, not an exact continuation of its frequency-dependent angular amplitudes. Representative source/characteristic grain27, guided thickness sampling and full-map travel are distinct approximations. Depth/peak numbers condition on this angular/boundary approximation as well as on the loss/source scenarios.

For receiver propagation Cu is per actual length (`alpha_E*length`), while Al remains a per-time lifetime (`transit/tau_E`). The heat/residence ledger assigns the numerical analog rates to absorption as a conditional scenario; the cited measurements do not deconvolve intrinsic and grain contributions. Explicit grain reflection is conservative redistribution; no second empirical grain-scattering rate is added. Neither scenario is a measured total-Pd-loss prediction or a bound.

Accepted slow histories and high-band catalogs share beam/intensity/record semantics, not an event-by-event reconstructed microscopic parent history. Their source ledger combines actual accepted low work with isolated-parent high-work expectations; it is not exact unresolved finite-record cross work. Same-time slow strain enters a declared one-way refraction sensitivity, without independent source energy.
