# Finite model and observable

## Variational foil

The governing model is a finite vector displacement space, not a phase-velocity ray histogram. With mass-normalized Legendre product functions, `u(r,t)=Σ φ_j(r) q_j(t)` and engineering strain `e=Bq`, the positive stiffness is `K=∫BᵀCB dV`. The finite Hamiltonian is `H2=(pᵀp+qᵀKq)/2`. Traction-free faces and edges are natural variational boundaries. Discontinuous rotated cubic tensors are integrated separately in explicit synthetic columnar grains. They modify the actual operator and signed displacement, not an arbitrary grain amplification. Hill isotropy preserves bulk modulus and the arithmetic average of Voigt/Reuss aggregate shear. Both controls use the same domain, retained frequency interval, projected-source energy convention and receivers; their actual eigenmode counts can differ.

This low-frequency representation contains collective bending/in-plane modes and both free faces in its weak operator. It does not resolve a nanosecond reflected wavefront, atomistic caustic, measured grain texture, or all acoustic branches. A finite polynomial space is the declared model; the eigen residual is not a continuum-convergence claim. Region averages below the supported lateral wavelengths do not resolve a correspondingly small hotspot. The primary physical exterior is a 24-mm-diameter cathode disk with flange mounting and roughly 1-cm² beam; the model's shape/boundary and support sensitivities must remain explicit.

## Physical-time marked source

The incident parent electrical rate is `I_true/e`, marked D+ with probability .85 and D2+ with probability .15. One molecular parent carries the sum of two saved per-nucleus host recoil energies, sharing a single parent sign; it is not two independent arrivals. Per-species positive and negative counts are independent Poisson with half the species rate, so their difference is an exact signed compound-Poisson count increment, including near-front cancellation when propagated. Effective signed z-traction packets are a declared coarse source model, not measured coherent yield or the net directed beam impulse.

Accepted G11 host power is already species- and 5%-FWHM-energy averaged. G11 saved only the central-energy species tracks, so central relative packet marks receive a recorded common correction to reproduce the accepted power exactly; this retains the first energy moment, not the complete beam-energy distribution. Source depth is the host-deposition first moment of each saved timeline. Timeline `depth_A` is Angstrom, while its terminal field of the same name is stored in metres. Thin beam stopping is not the deep loaded profile. The full fixed transport profile is consumed separately for D populations and temperature, without rescaling x or resetting exposure.

Each projected packet vector has unit mass-metric velocity norm, giving prescribed retained-band kinetic energy `eta_band*E_host,parent`. This fixes equal retained-band source energy rather than claiming equal unprojected microscopic traction. `eta_band=.01` is a named launch/band sensitivity; changing it rescales linear fields, while changing the quartic material coefficient requires its own conversion. Source sites are equal-area spatial quadrature, not a measured spot map. Continuous physical-time counts are binned at the stated dt; only their within-bin arrival phase is approximated. No conditioned arrival windows, time acceleration, periodic pumping or overlap-count duration is used. Five assumed energy lifetimes are discarded before the sampled exposure; the initial state is zero, not assumed thermal equilibrium.

## Local nonlinear receiver

For retained modes write `x=Ωq/sqrt(E0)`, `y=p/sqrt(E0)`. At the lateral center and depth **30 μm**, a normalized strain projection `a` is derived from actual **epsilon_xx**. This selects front-side bending strain rather than a near-neutral-plane thickness strain weakly driven by a normal beam. The localized positive Hamiltonian is

`H/E0 = (|x|²+|y|²)/2 + chi (a·x)^4/4`.

It couples modes through `dy/dt=-Ω[x+chi*a*(a·x)^3]`, `dx/dt=Ωy`. In the absence of forcing and damping this Hamiltonian is conserved analytically, and the modal quadratic energies can exchange. The implementation uses exact harmonic rotations with symmetric quartic kicks. The `chi=0` control uses identical realized parent counts. `chi=.3,1` are sensitivities, not imported optical Kerr coefficients. The reported dimensionless quartic strain coefficient gamma satisfies `Hnl=C11*Vloc*gamma*epsilon_xx^4/4`, with `Vloc=(2.5 mm)^2*(50 μm)`; gamma is derived from the actual mode normalization and `E0=P_band*tau_energy`. It is not measured Pd elasticity. The single-parent comparison uses one actual effective D+ packet and the same coefficient as the continuous-source run; it separates single-arrival nonlinearity from any across-arrival collective response. The nonlinear term is localized but acts on actual foil modes; it is not a full-foil nonlinear constitutive PDE.

## Quadratic receiver and thermal comparison

Every physical receiver uses `u_R=(2V_R)^-1∫_R eᵀCe dV`, including spatial cross correlations. It is not the square of the average strain. The receiver's modal form in x coordinates is `u_R=E0*xᵀA_R*x/2`. At harmonic classical thermal equilibrium in the identical retained band, `mean=kBT*tr(A_R)/2` and `sd=kBT*sqrt(tr(A_R²)/2)`. The mode-count proxy `(tr A)^2/tr(A²)` follows from those correlations; it is not an imposed independent volume-cell count. This harmonic thermal comparator remains separately labeled in nonlinear arms; it is not the nonlinear Gibbs distribution. Classical statistics are adequate in this sub-MHz band, where h*f is far below kBT.

Tail tables report time fractions and connected complete excursions at fixed receiver/height, with boundary-censored excursions separate. Duration is a contiguous observable crossing sampled at dt, not source age. Realizations are independent Poisson records, while successive samples are correlated. `-log(.05)/exposure` is a zero-event Poisson *rate-model* upper limit, not an exact distribution-free confidence bound for correlated excursion processes. Peaks and empirical tails are finite observations; six histories do not establish a universal tail law or vendor-Rayleigh distribution. The explicit receiver-bank union counts each time once, rather than summing spatially overlapping windows. It covers only that receiver bank; no whole-disk spatial event multiplicity is inferred.

## Conditional backwards map

The 0.6423 meV/D value is retained full-loading context only. Acoustic storage per local D is not stored D optical energy. Local conversion is separately conditional on a transfer fraction and an energy holding lifetime; no strain-to-D calibration is performed.

The direct hemisphere attenuation family reuses the accepted `depth.py` identity `K(L,z)=E1(z/L)-E1(sqrt(z²+R²)/L)`. Its local conversion scales as `eta_launch*eta_D*P_host*K(L,z)*tau_D/(A*L*n_D)`. It vanishes at L→0 for positive depth and at L→∞ although geometric density saturates; therefore larger attenuation length alone cannot grow conversion monotonically. The optimum and residual factor are explicitly tabulated. This attenuation family is distinct from the bounded modal foil's uniform energy-lifetime sensitivity, not a proof about mounted foil losses. A finite sampled nonlinear peak ratio is only a named multiplicative envelope when placed on that family; no extrapolated strength that reaches the reference is asserted.

Joint rows satisfy a stated product relation after optimum attenuation and the sampled nonlinear envelope. They are a Pareto tradeoff between concentration and holding time. The symmetric sum of squared log factors is an explicit optional cost proxy, not a universal cheapest physical design. Fractions in that backward family are set to their maximum 1; smaller fractions increase the requirement reciprocally. Any holding-time extrapolation longer than observed excursions must be read as a requirement, not an achieved duration.

### Finite-event holding and nonlinear-energy bound

The reported scan label chi=.3 or 1 refers to the canonical PdD 2.5-keV source at eta=.01 and tau=50 μs. Actual chi rescales with E0 so the **same dimensional quartic strain coefficient gamma** is retained across both nominal materials, energies and source/lifetime sensitivities at a fixed operator/band. `models.csv` records both chi values and gamma.

For any nonnegative quartic coefficient and fixed total Hamiltonian budget H0, positivity gives `u_R <= lambda_max(A_R)*H0`, independently of the nonlinear dynamics. `region_peaks.csv` uses the explicitly conditional budget H0=P_band*tau; that is a declared reference energy, **not an upper bound on all Poisson-driven realizations** (some sampled energies exceed this mean scale). Increasing a positive quartic coefficient does not remove that bound. Its conversion/residual columns retain the named local quadratic-loss law.

`finite_event_requirements.csv` consumes the longest complete observed excursion above four times each receiver's mean. It puts the observed global peak at that receiver throughout that duration, a generous envelope rather than an assertion that both extrema coincided. At transfer=1 and constant conversion rate r, an initially empty D receiver stores `r*tau_hold*(1-exp(-T_event/tau_hold))`, tending to `r*T_event`, not infinity, as holding grows. If that cap is below the reference, the table reports the finite residual and no holding-only solution **for this event envelope**; it says nothing universal about long-time accumulation from the continuing background. `finite_event_joint_frontier.csv` reports concentration versus holding under this finite-duration relation, separately from the direct-family steady frontier. No unobserved longer event is inferred.

Duration-survival columns count complete events lasting at least 1, 10 and 50 μs at each height, with the same exposure denominator. `receiver_bank_union_tails.csv` uses a dimensionless bank statistic `max_R(u_R/mean_R)` normalized to its own bank mean; it does not label that statistic J/m³ or assign it an artificial thermal comparator.
