# G12 residual: observations and reported conditions

`observations.csv` is a lossless join of the admitted study blocks by source row ID, not a pooled response. `measurement_block` separates D–D, D on Li, p on solids, and foil-state controls or characterization. Cvetinović compared hard and soft Pd using fluorine/lithium-induced reactions, not D–D; the hardness comparison is not a documented intervention on the same foil. Within each block, group by `study_id`, `channel`, `observable_kind`, `observable_unit` and any `reference_group` before comparing. No residual, covariate imputation or profile change is performed here.

- **Reference and dependence.** Davis has 48 measured rows plus a shared normalization anchor per target. Yuki has a different anchor and target preparation. Raiola’s S factors are inferred from thick-target slopes using stopping and detector corrections. Czerski detected yield is per deuteron current and collection time. Chen compares neutron-rate time bins between loading regimes on the same target family, not measured cross sections. Keep per-bin, saturation and phase-ratio records statistically dependent. Study notes identify overlays, source reanalyses and separate reference checks.
- **Conditions.** Blank means unavailable/inapplicable at the recorded scope, never zero. `study-context.csv` has lower/upper bounds, sample properties, setpoints and model assumptions as reported, scoped to a named study/series. Do not join these onto all points as if directly measured. Davis `cup_IV_*` is a calibration curve shared across hosts, not actual point current; Chen electrochemical current is not the high-voltage plasma current. Anneal/implantation protocol is not the operating temperature or in-run D loading. `ion_range_nm` is not an invitation to substitute a modeled surface or diffusion length.
- **Author fits.** Screening energies, enhancement ratios and limits are explicitly labeled. They are not independent measured points, instrumental calibrations, local impact energies or candidate regressors against the response used to infer them. Do not use a study’s reported fit to manufacture a nominal `E_cm_keV`.
- **Uncertainty.** Uncertainties follow the source and source notes; not all fields are one standard deviation. No common covariance model exists. Missing digitized bars stay unavailable. Where there is no exact trace, text supplies the published condition with scope rather than a fabricated point value.
- **Scope corrections at the join.** `reported_inferred_D_per_metal` preserves report-level, model-derived or approximate average loading; `reported_series_current_uA` preserves a stipulated setting. Neither is a point-specific measurement. The source blocks remain byte-identical in `inputs/`. The D–³He experiment and Cvetinović reaction controls remain separate; the Kasagi loading-density diagnostic and NMR Knight shifts are characterization, not fusion-response points.

## Three omissions that limit discrimination

1. **Same-run state of the target.** At the Davis point level, no paired D concentration/depth/time history or operating foil temperature. Independent surface chemistry, strain, trapping and local loading histories would distinguish a change in participating D population from a change in the reaction at fixed population.
2. **Actual impinging beam history.** No Davis per-point delivered current/density, cumulative charge or synchronized waveform, or equivalent pulse/collision observables. Nominal energy and its cup I–V calibration move together; conditioning on that shared curve cannot identify a separate current, flux or edge effect. Stopping/range estimates do not resolve this either.
3. **Comparable response and experimental lever.** The common covariance of the Davis reference, exact channel overlap, and matched-count/run ledgers are unavailable publicly; cross-study S factors, detected thick-target yields and electrochemically driven rates have different comparators. The Chen loading intervention is at 15 keV CM and changes retained D, not an ultrafast intervention on an otherwise matched foil. These records do not identify an impact transient as the source of the Davis residual.

`coverage.csv` gives nonblank field counts by study, observable and evidence class. It counts reported fields, not independent samples. Root `adapters/compile_external.py` uses pinned EXFOR decks and established Czerski digitizations. `adapters/compile_chen_curve.py` uses an SVG extracted from the publisher PDF (exact command in its docstring). `adapters/merge_tables.py` joins the reported observations and refuses duplicate source row IDs. `davis/README.md` gives primary supplement provenance. `sources/` records external source editions, origins, hashes and extraction limits; original PDFs and embargoed logs are not in this intake.

The exact intermediate CSVs in `inputs/` and the Davis CSV in `davis/` are compiler inputs. For analysis use `observations.csv` alone rather than stacking it with those same rows.
