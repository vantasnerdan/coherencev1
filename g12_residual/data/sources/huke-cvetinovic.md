## Berlin: same-target monitor, not a bare-yield measurement

- The recovered thesis supplies exact **p+t screening-series** data in Tables 4.10–4.14 (Ta-I 12, Sr 7, Li 4, Zr 14, Al 14 rows) and Tables 5.6–5.13 (Ta-A 21, B 10, C 21, D 18, E 20, Pd 23, C 8, Al-C 11). Each count includes one 25-keV lab anchor, set to unity by construction. The printed nominal 0.001 or 0.005 is noted on each anchor but is not an independent measurement error. Repeated energies have separate stable printed row IDs. These tables replace the corresponding points in EPL 2001 Fig. 3, Huke 2007 Fig. 11 and Huke 2008 Figs. 3, 5 and 7, rather than duplicating those panels. Auxiliary spectra, angular/branching measurements and implantation diagnostics are not substitutes for enhancement points.
- Huke 2008 §II Eqs. (1)–(6), thesis §4.3.2 and §5.3.3: `F_norm = y(E_lab)/y(25 keV lab)`. `y` is the thick-target experimental yield divided by the gas-target cross-section integral weighted by `1/sqrt(E)`; the two reduced yields in the ratio are from the **same preparation**. Campaign I above 25 keV chains adjacent jumps back to the monitor, whereas campaign II bracketing is also used above 25 keV. This is not directly `Y_meas / Y_model` or the thin-target cross-section enhancement. Czerski 2001 pp. 452–453 explicitly links the measurements to the proton channel, with neutron-channel measurements separate. The thesis `m`/`a` annotation identifies molecular D₂⁺/atomic D⁺ beam; tabulated energy is lab **per deuteron**, converted with the explicitly sourced factor of two to c.m. Reference E is 12.5 keV c.m. No further plasma-drop correction is applied.
- Ta-A–E are distinct runs: small C excess, slight C traces with C/O enrichment indicated, thick C layer, thick oxide, and slight C traces, respectively (Huke 2007 Fig. 11; Huke 2008 Fig. 5; thesis pp. 240, 248). The listed `p_t` values are initial implantation pressures only, not individual enhancement-point conditions. Pd was implanted short of saturation and exhibits C traces. The C and Al-C controls are distinct active carbon films; `Al-C` is not pooled with the bare Al foil. Bare-metal foil gauge, exact point-level beam settings, measured point D profiles and other unknowns are blank. The visibly heated 7-µm Ta foil in Huke 2008 Fig. 6(c) is a separate diagnostic, not the gauge of a screening series. Na had an oxide that precluded U determination (Huke 2008 Table I); neither it nor any missing fit central value is imputed.
- Fitted energies are never assigned a beam-energy point. Identical retellings are not repeated, but explicitly distinct estimates and one-/two-/four-parameter or layered-model alternatives are marked as fits on the same data, not independent repeats. Huke 2007 Table 2 trials 4–5 and thesis Table 5.14 trials 3–4 fixed U=0, so they are not fitted-U observations. Ta-D's 0.225±0.001 keV estimate and 0.433±0.130 keV oxide-layer variant are different fits to the same measurements. The authors’ Sr interval is captured as two endpoints, not a central estimate or 1σ. The two published Li estimates are not forced to agree. The Czerski 2004 Pd fit uses a reported nominal PdD₀.₂; Huke 2008 Table I instead gives the corresponding campaign's approximate D/Pd of 0.3, and an identical foil or loading history is not assumed. Quoted published fit errors do not encode the surface systematic.

## Cvetinović: three different reactions and observable conventions

- **Fig. 5 p. 4, 9 points** — normal-kinematics ¹⁹F(d,p)²⁰F S factor (GeV·b), measured in Garching on 8 and 85 nm CaF₂ targets. Point-to-thickness mapping not provided. Bare reference model Eq. (5) is `S(E)=19380−4596E−3218E² MeV·b` with `E` in MeV c.m.; this model is not added as fictitious S-factor points. Fig. 5 measured bars are statistical only; the red 95% band is for the fit, not measurement errors. The highest-energy error cap cannot be independently resolved from the marker even in the publisher JPEG; blank does not mean zero.
- **Fig. 6 p. 5, 18 points** — inverse-kinematics ²H(¹⁹F,p)²⁰F integrated enhancement (6 hard Pd, 6 soft Pd, 6 averaged from 94 and 138 nm CD₂ foils, the latter Au-coated). The bare-reference model uses the same-energy normal-kinematics Eq. (5) S factor; the adjacent 7.671 MeV lab controls correct H/D loss and are **not** the plotted denominator. For CD₂ the article gives an averaged lower panel, not a per-foil integration/normalization recipe or numerical Ue; do not treat it as a thick Pd series. Hard and soft fits (18.2±3.3 and 3.2±1.9 keV) are separate.
- **Fig. 8 p. 5, 34 points** — unratioed thick-target ¹H(¹⁹F,αγ)¹⁶O yield in counts per µC, 17 per foil. **§3.2 Eq. (8), p. 6** separately reports hard/soft fitted resonance strengths, 1.53±0.05 at 323.31 keV c.m. Soft is the authors’ bare proxy; they report a thin oxide that broadens its resonance. The resulting Ue=18.7±1.5 keV is distinct from the ratio. The text prints the equivalent resonance lab value in the wrong unit (6417.9 MeV, in a 6.410–6.819 MeV beam scan); no lab value is entered in that row.
- **Fig. 10 p. 6, 6 points** — thick-target ¹H(⁷Li,α)⁴He hard-foil reaction yield divided by soft-foil yield at matched energy; soft is the authors’ proxy, not a measured zero-screening curve. The hard fit is 2.86±0.19 keV. Table 2 NMR values are companion foil characterizations relative to water, never reaction rates or derived electron densities.
- Marker centers and cap ends came from the PDF vector paths for Figs. 6, 8 and 10, calibrated on printed linear ticks and ordered by energy within each series. The triangles use the cap midpoint for the plotted ordinate (geometric bounding midpoint of a triangle is offset). Fig. 5 used pixel centers and cap ends from the 1542×1158 publisher JPEG with x=177→1508 for 0.2→1.0 MeV and y=999→22 for 11→20 GeV·b. The remaining source plots are depth/SIMNRA and NMR characterizations or representative event spectra, not additional excitation-series points. Published bar coverage is not specified outside Fig. 5.
- **Target scope:** source calls these Chempur **soft Pd** versus **cold-rolled hard Pd**, not a documented manufacturing annealing schedule or an intervention on the same foil. Both 100 µm foils underwent the same gas cycling/unloading heat and reload in the mixed gas. §2 gives maximum H(D)/Pd of 0.70/0.47; Table 2 calls these `nD` but §4 calls them H/Pd. They are recorded in notes, not promoted to actual isotope-specific D/Pd. The 1-bar/24 °C preparation is not the experiment's operating pressure/target temperature. Beam currents are approximate series settings, and the two endpoints of the calculated ion ranges do not give per-point range. No isolated annealing effect, source-independent foil ratio, conductivity, or density is inferred.
