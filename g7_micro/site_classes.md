# Site-density intersections for the next electronic-structure comparisons

`python -m g7_micro.site_targets` joins the saved Ti/Pd occupancy bands, UC Davis depth bands, feasible depth-cell segments and modeled fuel columns. `site_targets.csv` is the full table, including missing or unsupported crossings. Intervals are surviving **sampled** shifts, not confidence limits, predictions of site screening, or continuous/extrapolated bounds. Extensive scope/units are part of each row.

- **Raiola Ti, D+D:** the displayed curve uses the 5 keV CM penetration proxy and hard upper limits, profiling binding on a 0–0.5 eV grid with descriptive Δχ²≤1. The JSON also retains 3.85 and 10 keV CM sensitivity. Exact Ti spectrum weights are unavailable.
- **Cvetinović Pd, F+D:** hard and soft capacities, and their difference, are separate curves; the band takes the published marginal ±1σ shift rectangle and a common binding energy in [0,0.5] eV. It is not a calibrated joint confidence region. The table uses the 500 keV CM response pivot; the JSON has both endpoints of the measured beam range. Generic absolute capacities cannot constrain a hard-minus-soft difference. Fluorine targets used initial H(D)/Pd, 85/15 D/H charging gas, and interleaved yield normalization for loss during irradiation. These are not D+D shifts. The soft target is commercial; the hard foil was rolled; preparation and loading co-vary.
- **UC Davis PdD/TiD, D+D:** each point is a necessary occupied-site demand, not the available capacity. The full-shape comparison uses the volume-only lower envelope from ratio-compatible cells within Δχ²=4 of the separately profiled one-foil minimum at cup-current power 1, excluding the sheet on the nominal D+D 1 keV CM profile support. A surviving capacity could exceed that required volume inventory; the fit may still need high screening in the separate sheet. The ratio-only and subsurface diagnostics instead use a conservative bound from the separately minimized \(f_s,f_b\) components and the total lower edge minus the entire eligible sheet. For the subsurface-only test, the central-ratio segment must include `f_b=0`; a full-shape pass anywhere on that segment is necessary, not sufficient, at `f_b=0`. No fit identifies a matched phase or an available-site density.

A part-(b) BO curve should show whether a relaxed site **retains D** and how the interaction changes as a function of separation. The keV values above are **effective penetrability shifts** in a beam model; they are not required values of the BO lowering at a single radius. A matched barrier-action calculation over its supported domain is required to compare the two. A measured local site count anchors a capacity curve only when the class, preparation, sampling depth and occupancy are attached; a single total D count or an unrelated host does not pin the point.
